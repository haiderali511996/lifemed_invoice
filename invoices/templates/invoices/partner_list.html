Should Pay In is each partner's share of what is left after customers have paid — not of the gross spend. Money the business has already collected pays for itself; asking the partners for it a second time would put in more than the business ever needed. Their share of the gross is shown alongside it for reference.

Suppliers are counted as paid the day the stock arrived. What is still owed to them is not recorded anywhere in this system, so a business buying on credit needs less cash in hand than this suggests.
